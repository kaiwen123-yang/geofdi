# Title options (working; the name is not frozen)

**Working title in `main.tex` (option 1):**
> GeoFDI: Fault and Slip Detection as Invariance Testing, with Finite-Sample Guarantees for Legged and Wheeled-Legged Robots

Rationale: names the method (GeoFDI), the reframing (detection *as* invariance testing), the differentiator
(finite-sample guarantee), and the scope (both of our own platforms). Long, but every clause is load-bearing.

**Option 2 — shorter, guarantee-forward:**
> Invariance Testing for Legged Robots: Exact False-Alarm Control for Fault and Slip Detection without Fault Data

Rationale: leads with the two things reviewers ask for first (exact FAR, no fault data). Drops the wheeled-legged scope
and the method name; better if the name is dropped or changed.

**Option 3 — thesis-forward:**
> What a Healthy Robot Must Satisfy: Symmetry as the Nominal Model for Fault and Slip Detection

Rationale: states the idea rather than the machinery, and reads as a position/journal paper. Weakest on searchability
and says nothing about the guarantee; keep as the fallback if the framing is judged more novel than the statistics.
